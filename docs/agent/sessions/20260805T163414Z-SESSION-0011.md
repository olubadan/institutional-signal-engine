## Counter-semantics correction — 20260805

- Renamed `quotes_superseded` to `current_state_overwrites`. It counts slot
  replacement operations, not unique quote identities, and therefore may
  overlap a quote later consumed for classification or current state.
- `quotes_received` counts every parsed quote message; `quotes_consumed`
  counts unique quote identities recorded in the consumed-input audit; and
  `quotes_pending_at_shutdown` counts unique latest-slot quote identities not
  consumed at shutdown. The lifecycle invariant is
  `quotes_consumed + quotes_pending_at_shutdown <= quotes_received`.
- The regression test proves both the invariant and intentional overwrite
  overlap. No report presents overwrite operations as a mutually exclusive
  lifecycle total.
